# Tenlux Roadmap

## Near term

- full visual QA for the current build
- more repeatable runtime validation
- polish around first-run and tray onboarding

## Next likely product wins

- richer automation conditions
- better multi-monitor awareness
- more detailed wallpaper preset management
- cleaner packaging / release identity

## Long-term direction

- keep Tenlux small, focused, and tray-first
- treat background footprint as a product feature
- make theme switching feel like a native desktop behavior, not a bolted-on tool
